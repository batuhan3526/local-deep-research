## Other problems

**Factual errors and misreadings (outside the 12, verified):**
1. **A battle from a different war is used as the 1683 battle order.** S1, the 12 Sept section (line ~139): "Osmanlı cephe yapısında, batı kanadı Nişava-Gola Çuka savunma hendekleri yakınında konumlanmış olup, Anadolu, Sivas ve Karaman beylerbeylerinin birimlerinden oluşuyordu", citing doi 10.34298/ic2574281r. That article is Ranđelović & Çekmez, "The Austrian Conquest of Niš 1689" (Istorijski časopis 74, 2025). Nišava and Gola Čuka are near Niš in Serbia, and the article does not discuss Vienna 1683 at all. The beylerbey line-up is not in the article either.
2. **A fabricated "Walpurgis Night" mutiny.** S1 (line ~181): "29 Temmuz 1683'te 'Walpurgis Gecesi' olarak bilinen isyan … Yeniçeriler … silahlarını Sadrazam'a çevirmişlerdir". The only source is the AI podcast fexingo ("the mutiny of July 29th, known as Walpurgis Night in the Ottoman context"). Walpurgisnacht is 30 April, and no Ottoman or Western scholarship records a janissary uprising against Kara Mustafa on 29 July 1683.
3. **The wrong sultan is deposed.** S3 (line ~644): "1687'de Sultan Suleiman II'nin askeri başarısızlıklar nedeniyle tahttan indirilmesi ve hapsedilmesi". In 1687 Mehmed IV was deposed and Süleyman II **enthroned**. Süleyman II reigned until his death in 1691. The sentence also contradicts the one before it ("IV. Mehmed … dört yıl daha iktidarda kaldıktan sonra tahttan indirilmiştir").
4. **Where the Sultan was during the siege.** S3 (line ~638) says that after the hand-over the Sultan neglected affairs "Edirne'deki sarayda … avcılığa öncelik vermiş" (fexingo). Belleten, cited in the same paragraph, says he handed over command at Belgrade and "avlanmak üzere Belgrad'da kalmayı tercih etmişti". S3 line ~618 (edgechat) also puts him in Belgrade. This is an internal contradiction.
5. **Podcast hearsay presented as a primary record.** S1 (line ~179): "Nusrat kayıtlarında, 12 Eylül 1683 sabahı dizanteri nedeniyle birçok Sipahi süvarisinin atlarına bile binemediği belirtilmiştir". The only source is the podscan transcript. "Nusrat" is a garbling of Silahdar's *Nusretnâme*, which nobody consulted. The same podcast's "500 mil" and "Yeniçerilerin yaklaşık üçte biri kaçmış" (it says "maybe a third") go into an S1 metric table as hard values; the hedges are dropped.
6. **Pipeline artefacts left in the text.** "[UNSUPPORTED: No evidence lines provided …]" appears 5 times. The S3 intro reads "Ancak mevcut kanıt satırları bu iddiaları desteklememektedir."
7. **The 1684 aftermath paragraph is confused** (S6, line ~1551): "Kuşatmanın başarısızlığı ve ardından gelen Kahlenberg Savaşı" sits in the 1684 Buda paragraph and cites a buymeacoffee page. Kahlenberg (1683) came before Buda (1684). The "Anında Sonrası" section is mostly about the 1684 Buda siege rather than the 1683 aftermath.
8. **Minor:** "Osmanlı kuvvetlerinin 15 Eylül 1683'e kadar Viyana'dan geri çekilmesi" (S1). The army broke and fled on the night of 12 Sept; Belleten has Kara Mustafa at Yanık (Győr) on 14 Sept. The execution is attributed to "Janissary Aghası", copying en.wiki's "by order of the Janissary Agha". Belleten says the Sultan ordered it, persuaded by Kızlarağası Yusuf Ağa. These are disputed rather than wrong.
