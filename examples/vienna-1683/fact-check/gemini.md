# Verification: Gemini Deep Research report, "1683 II. Viyana Kuşatması'nın Başarısızlık Nedenleri"

## Selection rule as applied
I walked the report from top to bottom. A claim qualified if it carried a citation number and contained a number, date or quantity, or a *concrete, checkable* act by a named person. I skipped vague characterisations such as "KMP saw an opportunity" or "his autocratic style caused tension". I took at most 3 claims per numbered section. The untitled preamble is not a numbered section, so it was excluded. The quota of 12 was filled by sections 1–4 (3 each), so sections 5 and 6 are not in the sample. I spot-checked them separately under "Other problems".

## Other problems
- **18 leftover "[cite: N]" artefacts**: [cite: 9]×5, [cite: 10]×4, [cite: 8]×2, [cite: 12]×2, [cite: 16]×2, and [cite: 1], [cite: 14], [cite: 31] once each. Most of them sit in the "Kaynak URL" columns of the tables.
- **Duplicate sources counted as two**: [6] DergiPark 1440563 and [8] Belleten are the same Çolak & Aydar 2020 article. The tables present them as independent support (e.g. "DergiPark (1440563) 6 … Belleten (2020) 8").
- **Internal contradictions:**
  - (a) March length: "4 ila 5 ay" in §3, but the §6 chronology runs 1 Apr → 14 Jul, about 3.5 months.
  - (b) Time window: "Temmuz ortasından Ekim" in the §3 text; "Mayıs-Ekim (~90 gün kuşatma süresi)" in the §3 table; "yaklaşık 90 gün" in §7. The actual siege lasted about 60 days.
  - (c) Relief army: "80.000" in the §2 text, "~80.000 – 120.000" in the §2 table.
  - (d) The Sultan's intent: stated as fact with "Yüksek" in §1, then described as historiographically disputed in §5.
  - (e) Heavy-gun claim: the text cites [15] (dokumen.pub) while the table cites [16] (Scribd).
- **Belgrade vs İstolni Belgrad**: the error appears twice (§1 and §6).
- **Spot checks outside the sample (§4–§6), lower confidence; please re-verify:**
  - §4 says Louis XIV "İspanya Veraset alanındaki iddialarından geçici olarak geri çekilmesi". This is anachronistic: the War of the Spanish Succession began in 1701. In autumn 1683 France attacked the Spanish Netherlands (War of the Reunions) rather than standing down.
  - §6 says Esztergom fell "Kasım 1683". My recollection is late October 1683, but I did not verify this.
  - §6 says the 1684 Buda siege was by "Kutsal İttifak orduları". It was essentially an Imperial/Habsburg army.
- **Garbled text**: "kalleşçe yağmalatarak", "fayılan", "Kahlenberg'de n yarma", "Komáro m".
