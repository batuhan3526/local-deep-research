# Fact-check: ChatGPT Deep Research report, "1683 Viyana kuşatması neden başarısız oldu?"

**Citation check.** The report's citations use the form `【63†L54-L61】`. These point into ChatGPT's internal search session, and the exported PDF gives no URL for any of them (its only link goes to chatgpt.com). A reader therefore cannot check whether the citations support the claims. This file checks factual accuracy only.

Some sources can be inferred from the wording. [38] closely paraphrases English Wikipedia "Battle of Vienna" (the 21 Jan / 6 Aug 1682 dates, "risky to launch in August or September 1682", "15-month gap", "roads and bridges"). The report even labels it "Viki kaynak". [50] appears to be Walter Leitsch, *History Today* 33/7 (1983), pp. 37-40. If that is right, the L37-L40 line numbers match the article's page range, which suggests the reference markers are not line numbers at all.

**Selection rule.** I read the report from top to bottom and took every claim containing a number, date, quantity or named-person action, with at most 3 per top-level section. The extracted text begins at section "II", so section I is missing from the export. The sections used were: II Askerî (3), Lojistik (3), Siyasi ve Diplomatik (3), Tarih Yazımı (3). That gave 12 claims, so "Kaynaklar Arası Çelişkiler" was not needed.

## Other problems (outside the 12 sampled claims)

- **Missing section I.** The document starts at "II. Viyana Kuşatmasının Askeri Başarısızlığı". Section I is absent from the export, and empty bullet lists ("• • • •") appear at the end of each page.
- **Anachronism and contradiction about the Holy League.** Section II calls the 1683 relief army "Kutsal İttifak'ın kurtarma ordusu". The Siyasi section then says, correctly, that the Holy League was formed "ertesi yıl (1684)" (5 March 1684). The report contradicts itself.
- **"Sultan Innocentius XI."** Pope Innocent XI is called "Sultan".
- **"III. Viyana Kuşatması'nın ardından."** There was no third siege of Vienna; this should be the II.
- **"Osmanlı doğudaki kazançlarının da geri verildiği."** The losses after 1683 (Hungary, Podolia, the Morea, under the Treaty of Karlowitz in 1699) were in the west and north, not the east.
- **Wrong sequence for the Treaty of Warsaw.** The report says "Savaş ilanından önce … (1683 Varşova Antlaşması)". The Ottoman war declaration was in August 1682 and the Treaty of Warsaw was signed on 31 March 1683, so the treaty came after the declaration.
- **"1683 sonbaharında kurulan müttefik ordu".** The relief army assembled at Tulln in early September 1683, which is late summer.
- **"Kara Mustafa … ağır topçu getirmeyerek".** Broadly correct. TDV gives 19 large mortars and about 120 field guns, and says Austrian artillery was stronger. However, "surları uzağa vuramıyor" is garbled.
- **Disease paragraph is garbled.** It begins with Ottoman camp disease, switches to dysentery "şehir içinde", and ends with "kuşatma altındaki garniyonu zayıfladı" (sic). That sentence makes Ottoman supply problems weaken the besieged garrison, which is a non sequitur.
- **"Osmanlı-Merzifonlu rejimi sarsıldı".** This is loose wording. Kara Mustafa was executed in Belgrade on 25 December 1683, and Mehmed IV was deposed in 1687. The report gives neither date.
- **Section "Kaynaklar Arası Çelişkiler".** The supposed contradictions come from mixing source types: the "büyük ve iyi teçhizatlı" army against the lack of heavy guns, and SultansTrail calling Leopold a coward. Neither is a real historiographic dispute; SultansTrail is a hiking-route website, not a scholarly source.
- **Historiography is thin and partly invented.** The "askeri vs lojistik/siyasi" schools are not tied to any real works. Stoye, Ágoston, Murphey, Kołodziejczyk, Abdurrahman Abdi Paşa and Silahdar are never cited. The İnalcık attribution has no reference. "Germen kuvvetleri" and "birleşik Haçlı ordusu" are loose.
- **Confidence labels.** Tags such as "Güven: yüksek (Viki kaynak/orta)" are given without any method. Section II's "yüksek/orta" covers claims that include the Lithuanian error.
